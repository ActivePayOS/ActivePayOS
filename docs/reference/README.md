# Reference documents

Source material used to verify ActivePayOS's pay modeling. These are official
government publications (U.S. Government works, public domain).

| Document | Source | Retrieved | What it covers |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| [DFAS-Guide-to-Understanding-Your-LES.pdf](./DFAS-Guide-to-Understanding-Your-LES.pdf) | DFAS via myPay ("Guide to Understanding Your LES", updated 2026-05-14) | 2026-07-27 | Definitions of every LES entitlement, allowance, and deduction type: pays and bonuses, BAH/BAS/allowances, taxes, SGLI, TSP fields, and the regulatory authority (Title 37 U.S.C. / DoD FMR 7000.14-R) behind each. |

When a pay figure or definition in the app needs a citation, prefer these
documents plus the live DFAS/IRS/state pages listed in `data/verification.ts`.
